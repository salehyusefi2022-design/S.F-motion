What I Do

Services

Four skills, one workflow — everything a podcast or YouTube channel needs to keep people watching.

Podcast Editing

Raw recordings cleaned up, paced, and mixed — filler words gone, levels balanced, silences tightened, so every episode sounds like it was recorded in one clean take.

YouTube Editing

Full-length videos cut for retention — pacing that respects the story, clean b-roll placement, captions, and a structure built to hold watch-time to the very end.

Motion Graphics

Titles, lower-thirds, transitions, and kinetic text built to match the energy of the content — animated with enough restraint to support the story, not distract from it.

Sound Design

Impact hits, whooshes, ambience, and music layered underneath the edit — timed to the cut so every beat lands and nothing feels flat or silent.

The Value

Why Editing Matters

A great recording still loses people if the edit doesn't hold them. Every awkward pause, flat cut, or slow intro is a moment where someone closes the tab — and that moment shows up directly in your watch-time, your downloads, and how the algorithm treats your next upload.

Editing isn't a finishing touch. It's the difference between content people scroll past and content people finish.

  • Higher Retention

    Tighter pacing and stronger hooks keep viewers watching past the first ten seconds — and all the way to the end.

  • A More Professional Brand

    Clean cuts, consistent sound, and polished graphics make a channel or podcast feel established from the first watch.

  • More Reach, Less Guesswork

    Platforms push content that holds attention — better retention means more recommendations and organic growth.

  • Your Time Back

    Hand off the timeline completely — raw footage in, a finished episode out, while you focus on recording and growing.
